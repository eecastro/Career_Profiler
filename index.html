What kind of work might you enjoy?

Rate 48 everyday work activities by how much you’d like doing them. You’ll get a three-letter interest code and a list of careers people with that code often enjoy.

  • Takes about 10 minutes.
  • Think only about whether you’d like the activity, not whether you’re good at it, have training for it, or how much it pays.
  • Completely anonymous: no names, no sign-in, nothing saved. Your answers never leave this device, and closing the page erases them.

    • Pick two or three careers that surprised you and read what a typical day looks like.
