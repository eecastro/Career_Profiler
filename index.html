What kind of work might you enjoy?

Rate 48 everyday activities by how much you enjoy doing them, or think you would. You’ll get a three-letter interest code and a list of careers people with that code often enjoy.

  • Takes about 10 minutes.
  • Think only about whether you enjoy the activity, not whether you’re good at it.
  • If you haven’t tried something, picture yourself doing it and go with your gut.
  • Completely anonymous: no names, no sign-in, nothing saved. Your answers never leave this device, and closing the page erases them.
